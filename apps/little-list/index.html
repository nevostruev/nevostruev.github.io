Little List

Grocery Planner for iPhone

A little less to remember on your grocery trips.

Keep a separate list for each shopping trip or destination. Quickly add what you need, check off what you buy, and pick up where you left off.

Your shopping, organized

Create color-coded lists, paste several items at once, and add notes. Sort items manually, by name, or by date bought. Move items between lists, clear bought items, and choose which lists count toward the app icon badge.

Copy the current list, all lists, or selected lists as a Markdown checklist with notes. Export follows your sorting and grouping choices, and you choose whether to include bought items.

A little personality for your lists

On-device AI can add an emoji to item names and assign grocery groups. Both run automatically for new items and name changes, with separate switches and customizable prompts in Settings. Show groups from the sort menu while automatic group assignment is on; turning it off hides groups and disables the grouping menu. Bought items stay together. Original names stay editable, and your lists remain usable when AI is unavailable. AI requires iOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence available.

Your lists, one shortcut away

Use Siri and Shortcuts to add items, see what’s left, and mark purchases bought. Build your own shortcuts to create a list, open a list, or export Markdown to another action. Choose a list for each action; new lists follow your default backup setting.

No Little List account required.
Lists stay on your device by default and work offline. For each list, choose private iCloud backup, individual invitations, or a reusable link that lets anyone with the link and an iCloud account join and edit. Shared lists include item activity. AI results and preferences stay on each device.

Enable “Backup to iCloud by Default” for new lists; existing lists keep their settings. Your iCloud lists are found automatically when you open the app. Pull down on a backed-up or shared list to refresh it. Offline edits sync when a connection is available.

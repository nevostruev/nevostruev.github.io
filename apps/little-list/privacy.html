Little List: Grocery Planner

Privacy policy

Effective September 20, 2026

This policy covers the Little List iPhone app, developed by Ivan Nevostruev.

Your lists stay on your device

Little List does not require an account or sign-in. Shopping lists, item names, notes, and app settings are stored locally on your device. The app does not send this information to the developer or operate its own cloud sync service.

You can delete items and lists in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data. Your device’s backup settings may allow Apple to back up app data; those backups are managed through Apple’s services and settings.

Photos and camera access

Adding items from a photo is optional. Little List requests camera permission only when you choose to take a photo. You can also select an image using the system photo picker without granting access to your entire photo library.

Photo recognition uses Apple’s Vision framework on your device. On iOS 26 or later, when Apple Intelligence is available, Little List also uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework to interpret recognized text and its positions into product names. This step uses the local system model, not a cloud model; photos and recognized text are not sent to a server for recognition. If the model is unavailable, the app uses basic Vision suggestions. Little List does not upload photos for recognition or save them with your shopping lists. Only the item names you confirm are added to your list. If you select a photo stored in iCloud, Apple may download it to your device through its photo services.

Photo issue reports

You can choose to share a recognition issue report through the system share sheet. The report includes a resized copy of the photo, the correct names you enter, original suggestions, any recognition error, whether model refinement was used or fell back, optional notes, and app and operating-system versions. Little List removes source photo metadata, including location, from the exported image. Visible details in the photo remain.

Nothing is sent automatically. You choose where to save the report or who receives it; sharing services handle it according to their own policies. If you send a report to the developer, its photo, labels, and feedback may be saved as test cases to improve recognition and check future changes. Contact little-list@nevostruev.com to request deletion of reports we hold.

Analytics and tracking

Little List includes no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. The app does not sell your personal information.

TestFlight testing

If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the developer. Feedback you submit is also shared, and some information may be associated with your name or email address. We use this information to troubleshoot and improve Little List, and retain it only as needed for that purpose. See Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice for details about Apple’s handling and retention of this information.

Contact and support

If you email little-list@nevostruev.com, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and retain it only as needed to provide support. You can use the same address to ask privacy questions or request deletion of support information we hold.

Policy updates

If the app’s privacy practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.

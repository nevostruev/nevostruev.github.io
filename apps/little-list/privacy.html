Little List: Grocery Planner

Privacy policy

Effective October 1, 2026 at 14:18 UTC

This policy covers the Little List iPhone app, developed by Ivan Nevostruev.

Local baskets and device settings

Little List does not require a Little List account. Baskets stay local by default. Little List uploads a basket to iCloud when you choose backup or sharing for that basket, or when you create it after enabling “Backup to iCloud by Default” on this device. This setting starts off, applies to new baskets including those created through Shortcuts, and does not enroll existing baskets. Turning it off leaves existing cloud baskets and pending backup requests unchanged. You can separately pass list information to Siri, Shortcuts, the clipboard, or another app through the actions described below. AI-enhanced names, AI groups, prompts, automatic AI preferences, ordering, colors, and badge preferences remain on each device, including for shared lists. The app does not send this information to the developer or operate a developer-hosted sync service.

You can delete items and baskets in the app. Deleting the app removes its local data. Your device’s backup settings may allow Apple to back up app data; those backups are managed through Apple’s services and settings.

Optional iCloud device sync and sharing

In builds with iCloud sync and sharing, open “Sync & Share This Basket” and choose Local, Back Up to iCloud, or Share with Others for the named basket. Back Up to iCloud maintains a private synchronized copy on devices using the same Apple account; it is not a separate versioned snapshot archive. No invitation to yourself or participant name is needed. Share with Others shows a name field and Apple’s invitation controls. Only invited people get access. Each basket has its own settings. At startup and when returning to the foreground, the app checks your Apple iCloud account and downloads baskets already backed up there or shared with you, including on a new installation. You can also run this check with “Find My iCloud Lists” in Settings. Discovery does not enroll or upload existing local baskets. Backup and sharing require an iCloud account. Baskets in Local mode are not uploaded.

Backup and shared records include the basket title and creation date, original item names, notes, bought status and timestamps, and activity identifying who added, changed, bought, reopened, or deleted an item. Existing bought items and notes are included. An iCloud user identifier and, if provided, your chosen participant name appear in activity. The participant name applies to shared baskets on this device. Private backups use your private iCloud database; others get access only through invitations. Pending backup requests and the default-backup preference are stored locally. Offline requests resume when the app can connect, and choosing Local before enrollment cancels the request.

AI processing, AI results and groups, prompts, and presentation preferences remain per device and are not included in shared records. Each device may display different AI enhancements for the same original item. Shared lists keep a local copy and a queue of offline edits. Synchronization runs while the app is open and when it returns to the foreground; delivery is not guaranteed to be immediate.

Closing an invitation sheet after creating a share does not stop sharing. Shared activity retains earlier field values and deleted-item information while the share exists. Choosing Local for an owned cloud basket asks for confirmation, removes the owner’s records from iCloud, ends participant access, and keeps a local copy; this requires an iCloud connection. Choosing Back Up to iCloud for an owned shared basket asks before removing other participants, including pending invitations, while retaining the private backup. A participant choosing Local leaves the original share and keeps a local copy; choosing Back Up to iCloud also queues that copy for private backup in their own account. Other devices on the same Apple account keep downloaded copies locally when they next refresh. Choosing “Delete Basket” for an iCloud basket asks for confirmation, then removes the owner’s iCloud share and the local basket, or leaves a received share and removes its local copy. The owner’s deletion ends sync and participant access; already downloaded or exported copies may remain. Once cloud removal is queued, the app saves the pending deletion locally and retries after reconnecting or relaunching. The basket remains on this device until cloud removal and the local save succeed. If it was the last basket, an empty local basket replaces it. Deleting a received basket or leaving through Apple’s sharing controls removes the participant’s local copy and unsynced edits. Switching its radio option to Local or Back Up to iCloud explicitly retains a copy. The owner and other participants keep access to the original. If access is revoked, previously downloaded information can remain on the participant’s device until they leave the list. Copies already exported or retained by others cannot be removed remotely. Deleting the app does not stop an existing iCloud share. Apple’s storage and backup services are subject to Apple’s policies and the user’s settings.

Clipboard export

In “Export,” you can choose the current basket, all baskets, or selected baskets and whether to include bought items. Tapping “Copy Markdown” copies the chosen content to the system clipboard, including basket titles, displayed item names, bought status, notes, and group names when grouping is on. You can paste that text into another app. Little List does not read the clipboard to perform this export or send the exported text to the developer.

Siri and Shortcuts

In builds with Siri and Shortcuts actions, you can add items, find lists and items, mark items bought or to buy, create a list, open a list, and export Markdown. These actions require the executing device to be unlocked. List and item choices expose names, storage labels, remaining-item counts, notes, and bought status to Apple’s Shortcuts system as applicable. Export returns displayed names, notes, and groups as text; it does not copy automatically. Results can be passed to other apps or services by the shortcut you choose to run.

Actions do not invite participants or change existing local lists to cloud lists. Create List follows the device’s default-backup setting, which is off initially. Changes to existing cloud lists follow their sync and sharing settings, including activity attribution. Reads use the copy saved on the device; offline changes wait for synchronization. Pending AI work from shortcut additions is saved locally and resumes when the app is active, subject to each device’s AI settings.

Apple handles Siri requests and Shortcuts according to your settings and its policies. Siri processing is separate from Little List’s on-device item enhancement; we do not promise that Siri requests or shortcut content stay only on your device. See Apple’s Siri and Dictation privacy notice and review the actions and destinations in shortcuts you run. Little List does not send shortcut content to the developer.

On-device AI item names

Automatic name enhancement is enabled by default, including after an app update. Adding an item or saving a changed original name queues that name and your saved prompt for processing by Apple’s on-device system language model. You can turn off “Automatically enhance item names” in Settings; this preference is stored locally, and turning it off stops pending automatic work without removing saved enhanced names. Turning it on does not process the entire existing library.

Choosing “Enhance names & assign groups” manually processes names and assigns groups across all your baskets, including bought items. Both features run even when their automatic switches are off. The Name prompt can be edited whether automatic enhancement is on or off. Closing Settings saves prompt changes and processes all existing items while automatic enhancement is on; when it is off, those changes save without running AI and apply to the next manual enhancement. The feature requires iOS 26 or later and available Apple Intelligence. Item names and your prompt are processed on your device, with no cloud-model fallback, and are not sent to the developer or an AI service for enhancement. If AI is unavailable or fails, saved original names remain usable. Original and enhanced names are stored locally; item rows display one name with a sparkle when enhanced.

On-device AI item groups

Automatic group assignment is enabled by default, including after an app update. Adding an item or saving a changed original name queues that name, your grouping prompt, and up to 50 existing group names for processing by Apple’s on-device system language model. This context helps reuse suitable categories. Assigned groups, the grouping prompt, and the automatic-grouping preference are stored locally. Groups are read-only in the item editor.

You can turn off “Automatically assign item groups” in Settings to stop pending automatic grouping while keeping saved groups. Turning it back on applies to future additions and name changes. “Enhance names & assign groups” updates names and groups for every item, including bought items, even when automation is off. Closing Settings saves a changed grouping prompt and reassigns groups while automation is enabled; while it is off, the prompt saves without running AI. Showing groups in the sort menu is a separate display setting, and bought items stay together.

Group assignment requires iOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence available. Processing takes place on your device, with no cloud-model fallback; item names, group names, and the prompt are not sent to the developer or an AI service. Failed assignment preserves saved groups; new or renamed items remain ungrouped until assignment succeeds.

App icon badge

You can choose which baskets contribute their remaining To buy items to the app icon badge. This preference is stored locally. When you first include a basket, Little List requests iOS permission for badges only. The app updates the count on your device; it does not send push notifications or request notification sounds or alerts. You can change badge permission in iOS Settings.

Photos in earlier alpha builds

The standard version does not include photo recognition or request camera or photo-library access. The following describes the optional photo feature in earlier alpha builds.

In those alpha builds, adding items from a photo is an optional feature, disabled by default. You can enable Camera under … → Settings; the preference is stored locally on your device. Little List requests camera permission only when you choose to take a photo. You can also select an image using the system photo picker without granting access to your entire photo library.

Photo recognition uses Apple’s Vision framework on your device. On iOS 26 or later, when Apple Intelligence is available, Little List also uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework to interpret recognized text and its positions into product names. This step uses the local system model, not a cloud model; photos and recognized text are not sent to a server for recognition. If the model is unavailable, the app uses basic Vision suggestions. Little List does not upload photos for recognition or save them with your shopping baskets. Only the item names you confirm are added to your basket. If you select a photo stored in iCloud, Apple may download it to your device through its photo services.

Analytics and tracking

Little List includes no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. The app does not sell your personal information.

TestFlight testing

If you install a beta through TestFlight, Apple collects crash logs and usage information and shares them with the developer. Feedback you submit is also shared, and some information may be associated with your name or email address. We use this information to troubleshoot and improve Little List, and retain it only as needed for that purpose. See Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice for details about Apple’s handling and retention of this information.

Contact and support

If you email little-list@nevostruev.com, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and retain it only as needed to provide support. You can use the same address to ask privacy questions or request deletion of support information we hold.
